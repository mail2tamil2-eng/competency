# Preview Course Features Guide

## How to Access Preview Features

### 1. **Open the Preview**
- Go to **Step 3: Generate Course** page
- Click the **"Preview Course"** button (blue button with eye icon)

### 2. **Available Features in Preview**

#### **Top Header Buttons:**

- **Menu Button** (≡ icon) - Click to open the course navigation menu
  - Shows all slides/topics in the course
  - Active slide is highlighted in orange
  - Click any slide to jump to it

- **Help Button** (? icon) - Click to open the help panel
  - Shows explanations of all player controls
  - Describes navigation, transcript, menu, and exit functions
  
- **Exit Button** (⎋ icon) - Close the preview and return to Generate Course page

#### **Bottom Player Controls:**

- **CC Button** - Click to toggle Closed Captions overlay
  - When active: Shows orange background
  - Caption text appears at the bottom of the video area
  - Click again to hide captions

- **Transcript Button** - Click to open transcript panel
  - Slides up from the bottom
  - Shows full course transcript with scrollable content
  - Click X to close or click Transcript again to toggle

#### **Navigation:**
- **Previous/Next buttons** - Orange gradient counter with arrows
- **Play/Pause** - Main play button
- **Progress bar** - Shows course progress (when enabled)
- **Slide counter** - Shows current slide / total slides

### 3. **Testing Each Feature**

**To test CC (Closed Captions):**
1. Open Preview
2. Look for the "CC" button in the bottom control bar (left side)
3. Click "CC" - it will turn orange
4. You'll see caption text appear at the bottom of the video area
5. Click "CC" again to hide captions

**To test Transcript:**
1. Open Preview
2. Look for the "Transcript" button at the bottom center (below the controls)
3. Click "Transcript" - the text will turn orange
4. A panel slides up from the bottom showing the full transcript
5. Scroll through the transcript content
6. Click X in the transcript header or click "Transcript" again to close

**To test Menu:**
1. Open Preview
2. Click the Menu button (≡) in the top-right header
3. A panel slides in from the left showing all course topics
4. Click any topic to navigate to that slide
5. Active slide is highlighted in orange
6. Click X to close the menu

**To test Help:**
1. Open Preview
2. Click the Help button (?) in the top-right header
3. A panel slides in from the left showing help information
4. Scroll through the help content
5. Click X to close the help panel

### 4. **Visual Indicators**

- **Orange color** = Active/Selected state
- **White/Gray** = Inactive/Default state
- **Borders with orange glow** = Modal panels have orange accent borders
- **Smooth animations** = All panels slide in/out with motion effects

### 5. **Tips**

- Only one modal panel can be open at a time (Menu, Help, or Transcript)
- CC overlay can be shown alongside modal panels
- All features are always available in preview mode for testing
- The info banner in the top-right disappears when you open any feature

## Feature Availability

These features are **always visible in the preview** regardless of the Player Element Controls settings in Step 3. This allows you to test all functionality before finalizing your SCORM package configuration.
